Who is it for?

MenTor is for all first-year male students, primarily between the ages of 19 and 25. From health-related programs to the business world, everyone can participate. If you're new, have concerns about how to navigate your transition to university, and would like some advice or guidance on where to turn depending on your situation, MenTor is for you.

What is the problem?

Did you know?

64%of Canadian men report moderate to high stress.
67%are not seeking professional mental health support.

Source: Canadian Men's Health Foundation, 2025

This is very real for male university students: new people, new schedules, a new school, several buildings to find, and more complex time management as they balance their personal lives, work and studies.

The problem is that, for men, asking for help is often seen as a sign of weakness and can feel embarrassing, and first-year students often do not know where to go. With the help of a student who has just been through it, it becomes much easier.

What is MenTor?

MenTor is a peer mentoring program for first-year men, aged 19 to 25. Each student is matched with a 2nd or 3rd-year mentor who has been through first year. We match you with a mentor from your program who shares your interests whenever possible, based on your sign-up answers.

Your mentor listens to you, offers advice based on his own experience, and shares his perspective on both academic and personal matters. If necessary, he will refer you to the appropriate university services. It is free, informal and confidential.

What do you get?

Someone who has been through it

The mentor understands your classes and your fears, and knows what the workload and exams are like.

A safe place to ask

No judgement, informal and confidential. Our mentors will be trained for it.

A guide

If you need more, your mentor points you to the right university service.

A friendly face on campus

Someone to talk to who understands your first year, and who could become a future university friend.

How does it work?

Sign up

Fill out the form below with your contact information, your program and your interests.

Get matched

We pair you with a 2nd or 3rd-year male student, based on your program and interests.

Talk

Whether it's through text messages, scheduled meetings, or activities with your mentor, you'll be able to ask him questions, seek his advice, and build a relationship with him.

Get pointed to help

If the mentor is unable to assist you with your situation, he will refer you to the appropriate university service, such as academic support or psychological services.

Questions

Is it confidential?

Yes, the conversations you have with your mentor are confidential.

What can I talk about with a mentor?

Courses, workload, exams, stress, or anything else on your mind.

Is a mentor a therapist?

No, the mentor is still a student just like you, and that is actually his strength. If you need a therapist or any other more specialized resource, he will guide you through the process.

How often do we meet?

You and your mentor decide together. Meetings can be short and flexible, in person or online.

What happens to my personal information (Law 25)?

We only use your details to contact you and to match you with the right person for this program.
